隐私政策（中文）

欢迎使用「简单记账」（英文名 SimpleLedger，以下称「本应用」）。我们非常重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理与您相关的信息。

1. 我们是谁

本应用由独立开发者提供，用于帮助您在设备上管理个人收支、账户、预算等信息。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

本应用不会要求您注册账号，也不会主动收集可识别您身份的个人信息上传到我们的服务器。

您在使用本应用时自行录入的内容，可能包括但不限于：

  • 账户名称、余额、币种等账户信息
  • 收支流水、转账、分类、标签、收款人/付款人、支付方式
  • 预算与常用模板
  • 应用设置（如语言、主题、默认币种等）

上述数据仅保存在您的设备本地（例如通过系统本地数据库与偏好设置存储）。

3. 我们如何使用信息

本地保存的数据仅用于向您提供记账、统计、预算等应用功能，不会用于广告定向，也不会出售给第三方。

4. 数据存储与传输

  • 本地存储：记账数据与设置默认保存在您的设备上。
  • 不上传服务器：本应用当前不设自有云同步服务，不会把您的账本内容上传到开发者服务器。
  • 系统能力：如您使用设备自带的备份、同步或分享功能（例如系统备份），相关处理由苹果公司或您选择的系统服务按其隐私政策执行，不在本应用控制范围内。

5. 第三方服务与跟踪

本应用当前不集成第三方广告 SDK、统计分析 SDK，也不使用跨 App 跟踪（ATT）。我们不会将您的数据出售或用于跨应用广告追踪。

6. 权限说明

本应用核心功能不依赖访问相册、通讯录、定位、麦克风、相机等敏感权限。若后续版本新增功能并需要相关权限，我们会在系统弹窗中说明用途，并仅在您同意后使用。

7. 数据控制与删除

您可以在应用内自行增删改自己录入的数据。若卸载本应用，设备上由本应用保存的本地数据通常会一并删除（以您的系统与备份设置为准）。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用面向一般用户，不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。若您是监护人并认为儿童向本应用提供了信息，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系。

9. 政策变更

我们可能适时更新本政策。更新后会在本页面公示新的「最后更新日期」。若变更对您的权利有重大影响，我们会尽可能通过合理方式提示。

Privacy Policy (English)

Welcome to SimpleLedger (Chinese name: 简单记账, “the App”). We respect your privacy. This Policy explains how information related to you is handled.

1. Who we are

The App is provided by an independent developer to help you manage personal expenses, accounts, and budgets on your device.

2. Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information to upload to our servers.

Content you enter while using the App may include, without limitation:

  • Account labels, balances, currencies, and related account details
  • Transactions, transfers, categories, tags, payees/payers, and payment methods
  • Budgets and templates
  • App preferences (language, theme, default currency, etc.)

This data is stored locally on your device (for example, via the on-device database and preferences).

3. How we use information

Locally stored data is used only to provide bookkeeping, insights, budgeting, and related features. It is not used for targeted advertising and is not sold to third parties.

4. Storage and transmission

  • Local storage: Your ledger data and settings are kept on your device by default.
  • No developer cloud upload: The App currently does not provide its own cloud sync service and does not upload your ledger content to developer-operated servers.
  • System features: If you use device backup, sync, or sharing features provided by Apple or other system services, those processes are governed by their respective privacy policies and are outside our control.

5. Third parties and tracking

The App currently does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) cross-app tracking. We do not sell your data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

6. Permissions

Core features do not require access to Photos, Contacts, Location, Microphone, or Camera. If a future version needs additional permissions, we will explain the purpose in the system prompt and use them only with your consent.

7. Your control and deletion

You may add, edit, or delete data you entered in the App. Uninstalling the App typically removes locally stored App data from the device, subject to your system and backup settings.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date on this page will be revised accordingly. For material changes, we will provide notice in a reasonable manner where practicable.

10. Contact us
